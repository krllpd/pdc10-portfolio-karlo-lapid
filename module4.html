4.1 The Lost Context Archive: Protocol Ground Truth

Goal

As a team, restore a corrupted archive by passing six chambers (State Reconstruction, Context, Evidence, Trust, Grounding and Citation) and a Final Archive Override. Each chamber tested how well we could keep track of the right facts and back up answers with evidence.

Archive evidence

Reflection

Chamber 1 showed how messy a real conversation can be. The student first asked about BSIT for AY 2025–2026, then corrected it to BSCS for AY 2026–2027, added that they were a working student, asked about a coffee shop, and even shared a password. We had to keep only the latest correct facts, drop the outdated ones, ignore the unrelated question, and never use the password.

The later chambers taught us to trust documented sources over unsupported claims, to not invent things like a deadline when the evidence doesn’t give one, and to protect restricted student information.

My biggest takeaway is that a good assistant doesn’t just answer. It keeps track of what is true right now, uses evidence, and admits when it doesn’t know instead of guessing.
